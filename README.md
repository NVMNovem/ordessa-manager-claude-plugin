# Ordessa Manager

Read and edit your Ordessa pickup shop's catalogue from a conversation with Claude.

## Use it

Connect the Ordessa Manager connector and sign in on Ordessa's page with your shop's
account. Then ask about your products, sections, ingredients, option groups and
prices, or ask for changes: reprice a listing, mark a product sold out, add a French
name, or apply many edits at once as one change that either succeeds completely or
changes nothing. Deleting anything asks for your confirmation first.

It acts as your own Ordessa account and can do nothing your role does not allow;
staff accounts can only read. Disconnect at any time in the Ordessa Manager app under
*AI assistants*.

## Data

The connector sends the tool calls Claude makes — a tool name and its arguments —
to Ordessa at mcp.ordessa.org, and returns catalogue data your account can see. It
never returns your customers or your orders, and Ordessa never receives the rest of
the conversation. See Ordessa's privacy policy for what is kept and for how long.
